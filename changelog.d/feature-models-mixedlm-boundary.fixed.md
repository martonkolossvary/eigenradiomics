The clustered continuous model (`groups=` with a continuous outcome) is now an exact REML random-intercept fit: a one-dimensional search over the variance ratio with closed-form terms per cluster. statsmodels 0.14.6 `MixedLM` stopped at a zero variance in about 28% of small simulated clustered samples whose REML maximum was above zero. At a zero variance it then returned fixed effects other than the pooled fit, and on Linux it could raise on a singular Hessian. The new fit gives the same answer on every platform and matches `MixedLM` where `MixedLM` reaches the maximum. Standard errors are the model-based GLS errors (as in lme4 and nlme). A fit with a zero variance keeps status "ok", equals the pooled fit, and says so in `warning`.
