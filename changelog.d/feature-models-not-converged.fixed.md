GEE, logistic, GLMM and Cox fits get the status `not_converged`, with the warning text in `error`, when the optimizer reports no convergence or emits a ConvergenceWarning, a PerfectSeparationWarning, or the GLMM text "did not converge". Before, such a fit got the status `ok`. These rows keep their estimates and stay in the FDR family, but `top_hits`, `bar`, `matrix` and the plots use only `ok` rows. A new `warning` column records a HessianInversionWarning or a zero REML random-intercept variance and keeps the status `ok`.
