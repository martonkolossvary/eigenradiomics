MixedLM, GEE, logistic, GLMM, and Cox fits get the status `not_converged`, with the warning text in `error`, when the optimizer reports no convergence or emits a ConvergenceWarning (but not the MixedLM boundary note), a PerfectSeparationWarning, or the GLMM text "did not converge"; before, such a fit got the status `ok`. These rows keep their estimates and stay in the FDR family, but `top_hits`, `bar`, `matrix`, and the plots use only `ok` rows; a new `warning` column records a boundary note or a HessianInversionWarning and keeps the status `ok`.
