When cluster labels are in use (also through MultiIndex inference), `compute_reproducibility` withholds the independent-row inference: the ICC F-test p-value and the two-reader Pearson/Spearman p-values and Fisher intervals are NaN. The ICC sheet adds `n_valid`, `n_clusters`, `n_readers`, `status`, `ci_method`, `ci_status` and `n_boot_valid`. A new `random_state` argument sets the bootstrap seed.
