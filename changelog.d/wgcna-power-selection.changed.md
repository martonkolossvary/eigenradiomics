`WGCNAReducer(soft_power="auto")` now selects the lowest power whose scale-free R² is above `r_squared_cut`, whose mean connectivity is at most `mean_cut`, and whose slope is negative. When no power meets these criteria, it uses the best-R² power as before, but it now warns and sets `soft_power_criteria_met_` to False. Before, PyWGCNA fell back with no warning, and the documented ValueError never occurred. Set `soft_power_fallback="raise"` to stop instead.
